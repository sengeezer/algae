## Why It Surfaces

Differences reveal the pattern quickly, and GeeksforGeeks identifies the term as:

$$
T_n = n^2 + n^3 = n^2(n+1)
$$

## Worked Example

- `n = 2` gives `4 + 8 = 12`
- `n = 4` gives `16 + 64 = 80`
